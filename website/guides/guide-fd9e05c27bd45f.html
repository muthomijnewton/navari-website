A little know-how goes a long way.

Use Kitchen or /vunapos/kitchen to see preparation work as it arrives. A chef with only the kitchen role does not need to open a cashier shift.

Open the correct board

Sign in with an assigned kitchen user. Select the relevant station in the kitchen view. The board lists stations/tickets that user permissions allow for the Restaurant/Branch context, not only tickets created by the current chef.

Stations can receive orders from several POS Profiles in that same Restaurant/Branch. Kitchen Station and Branch restrictions may narrow what a chef sees.

Progress a ticket

Status Kitchen action / meaning
Sent A placed round has arrived; a kitchen user can Accept it.
Accepted Kitchen has acknowledged it; Start when preparation begins.
Preparing Work is in progress; Ready completes the required preparation/manufacturing steps.
Ready The items are ready to serve. A service user can mark Serve where appropriate.
Served Service is complete for the ticket; this is not itself proof of payment.
Cancelled Its order was cancelled through the permitted cancellation process.
  1. Open the ticket and check table/service, waiter, items, quantities, notes and modifiers.
  2. Accept it when the kitchen takes responsibility.
  3. Start preparation.
  4. Ready it only after the actual dishes are prepared and the required stock operations can complete.
  5. Service staff use Serve when the items have been handed over.

Only actions permitted by role and current state are shown. A waiter may view progress but is not automatically authorised to accept/start/ready tickets. Chefs do not collect customer payment merely because they can mark food ready.

The board refreshes as work changes. If another terminal already changed a ticket, refresh and use its current state rather than repeating a stale action.

Production errors

Ready is not just a coloured label. It completes the associated preparation workflow and can fail on ingredient availability, tracking, inspection or other ERPNext manufacturing checks.

Read the dish/recipe error, correct the cause through authorised stock/manufacturing staff and retry the same ticket action. A failure is recorded for review; do not create a second order to replace it blindly.

Do not complete a Work Order separately in Desk just to bypass an unclear error without checking its ticket and stock history. If someone already completed production, refresh and review the existing result.

Paper and billing

The KDS does not imply automatic paper printing at kitchen printers. Billing belongs to the cashier's visit workflow. A profile may allow the cashier to finish pending preparation during billing; see Bill, Cancel and Release Tables.
