A little know-how goes a long way.

This page is for suppliers using the portal at https://<your-site>/vuna-sourcing.

Overview

The Overview page is the first page you see after signing in.

  • Open RFQs, Open Orders, Outstanding and Paid (12 months) at the top.
  • Purchase Orders for the last 12 months, and Invoices by Status.
  • Your open RFQs and open orders, with links to each one.
  • Account Details: the details the buyer holds for you, such as type, supplier group, currency and tax ID.

Menu

Menu item What you find there
Request for Quotation Requests sent to you. See Quoting on the Portal.
Supplier Quotation Quotations you have drafted or submitted, along with their status.
Purchase Order Orders placed with you, including quantities received so far.
Purchase Invoice Invoices recorded against your orders.
Debit Note Debit notes raised against you.
Pending Invoices Outstanding amounts owed to you, due dates, and the number of days overdue.
Onboard / My Applications Apply for another role or check the status of previous applications.

Printing

Quotations, orders and invoices have a Print button that opens the document in the buyer's print format.

Live Bids

While you are taking part in an open bid, a Live bid panel sits in the corner of every page with the time left and your position. Click it to jump to the bid room. See Open Bids.

Switching Accounts

If your login is linked to more than one supplier, or to a supplier and a customer, use the account menu at the top left to switch between them.
