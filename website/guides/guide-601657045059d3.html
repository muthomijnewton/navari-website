A little know-how goes a long way.

Start with the symptom below, then use the linked guide. Keep the transaction reference and exact error when asking for help.

No POS Profile or unable to open a shift

Confirm the user is assigned to an enabled profile with an enabled Selling price list in the correct currency. Check payment accounts and whether that same user already has an open shift.

Open and Close Your Shift

Item is missing or has a zero/unexpected price

Check that it is enabled for sales, allowed by the profile/menu and priced for the selected list and UOM. Check the customer and quantity before changing the rate. Non-stock items may need Allow Service Items.

Find Items and Choose Units · Prices and Discounts

Insufficient stock even though pieces exist

Confirm the warehouse and conversion factor. One carton can require many stock pieces. Quantities across different UOM rows share the same stock.

Batch and Serial Allocation

Address is not offered

Check the selected customer, address link and the user's read/share permissions. Do not attach an unrelated customer's address.

Customers and Shipping Addresses

Checkout requires a field or approval

Complete the requested field for the current transaction type, or use the permitted workflow action. A disabled/hidden action may reflect the document state, not a network fault.

Checkout Fields and Approval Workflows

Paid transaction still appears unpaid or submission is unclear

Check whether the invoice/payment was actually submitted and allocated. A sent STK prompt is not payment confirmation. Do not collect money twice.

M-Pesa Integration · Connection and Submission Recovery

Return action is unavailable

Confirm profile permission, original invoice type/profile, submission status and remaining returnable quantity.

Return Items

Looking for a report

Use Sales History for transaction follow-up, Payments for receipts/allocation history and Close Shift for counter reconciliation. Use the appropriate ERPNext accounting/stock reports for formal ledgers; these guides do not imply a separate restaurant analytics suite.

Restaurant-specific problems

For missing menus, unavailable tables, ingredients, kitchen tickets or blocked bills, use Restaurant Troubleshooting.
