Number Cards

Use Number Cards to check your flock summaries at a glance. They normally use your default Company and the current month through today. Optional Company, Item, Batch, House, and date filters narrow their scope where configured. Dashboard eligibility uses livestock-marked Batches; verify the field before troubleshooting a zero.

Survival Percentage

Shows the operational period survival percentage, combining animal counts rather than averaging Batch percentages. It excludes separately classified culling from deaths. Read the dedicated survival guide for denominator and unavailable cases.

Feed Consumed

Sums stock quantities from submitted Feeding / Material Issue entries linked to eligible livestock Batches, converted to Kg. It does not count draft entries or purchases sitting in the feed store. A zero means no matching recorded consumption; an unavailable value can indicate invalid units, inaccessible details, or missing house allocation.

Read this value as kilograms of feed, not currency.

Feed Consumed card with a verified kg value.

Internal Feed Conversion Ratio

Feed Conversion Ratio is kg feed divided by kg live-weight gain between valid measurements. It is a ratio, not a percentage or currency. The shared interval rules are also used by the batch performance report.

For every active Batch/House in scope, the calculation uses the latest two measurements within the selected period. Those measurements must have different timestamps, explicit Company/House/Kg UOM, valid samples and positive average weights. The interval needs positive population, positive biomass gain, and no unsupported population-changing movements. Relevant feed must be posted in the interval. Several eligible house/batch intervals are aggregated as total feed divided by total biomass gain, not an average of ratios.

Example: 100 animals growing from 1.0 to 1.2 kg with 40 kg feed give FCR 2. One Weight Record cannot establish that gain. Missing or invalid intervals return unavailable/N/A with a reason; do not create an invented opening weight to obtain a number.
