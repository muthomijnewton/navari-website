Building Navari on grounded values & perseverance

The story of Muchai Gateru, Navari’s roots and the values behind the company.

In this story

The thread through this story

Build carefully, take ownership and grow alongside the community.

01

Roots in Meru

Muchai Gateru’s journey begins in Meru, where his parents and local entrepreneurs shaped his approach to work. Their influence runs through the company story: take responsibility, understand your capacity and do the work well.

02

Finding the right foundation

After a career in software development and Microsoft Dynamics, Muchai founded Navari in 2015. Integration challenges on a healthcare project later strengthened his interest in a connected platform, leading the business towards ERPNext and Frappe.

03

Growing with purpose

The published profile describes a company built through persistence, careful project selection and investment in its people. Community events and contributions such as local compliance and M-Pesa integrations form part of that approach.

The complete profile follows this journey in greater depth, including Muchai’s reflections and photographs from the Navari community.
