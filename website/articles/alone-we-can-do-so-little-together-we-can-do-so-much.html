Case study overview

Alone we can do so little, together we can do so much

A story of collaboration and ERPNext implementation at Meru Greens Horticulture.

NNavari Journal·18 Sep 2020

In this story

From farms to finished productsBringing the information togetherMaking the transition
← Back to the archive
The thread through this story

A shared system can connect work from farmer registration to the finished product.

01

From farms to finished products

Meru Greens Horticulture works with smallholder farmers to produce and market fruit and vegetables. Navari’s case study follows the processes behind its vegetable packaging operation, from planting inputs to production and distribution.

02

Bringing the information together

The original story describes fragmented farmer information, stock challenges and limited reporting. The implementation connects farmer records, purchasing, selling, production and inventory, using configuration and targeted customization.

03

Making the transition

The published account covers process mapping, migration, training, acceptance testing and support after launch. It also highlights the importance of a customer-side project champion and clear coordination.

Read the complete original case study for the detailed workflows and the implementation context at the time of publication.
