Choosing the Best ERP System in East Africa: Why ERPNext Stands Out.

A perspective on evaluating ERP systems and where ERPNext fits for businesses in East Africa.

NNavari Journal·8 May 2025

In this story

← Back to the archive
The thread through this story

Evaluate the fit between your processes, your implementation partner and the platform.

01

Start with the operating challenges

Navari’s article considers the problems businesses face when information is fragmented: limited visibility, manual work and disconnected departments. Those needs provide a practical starting point for evaluating an ERP system.

02

Consider the platform and its flexibility

The article highlights ERPNext’s connected business functions and open-source foundation. It also discusses adapting systems to regional requirements and supporting teams across locations.

03

Plan for implementation

Local consulting, configuration and support matter alongside product selection. A suitable implementation needs to address the organisation’s workflows and operating context, rather than treating software selection as the final step.
