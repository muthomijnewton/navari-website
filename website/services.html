A few useful answers

Before your next move.

Where should we start?

Start with the business process you want to improve. Navari can help map requirements and choose the right ERPNext modules or Frappe applications.

Is implementation included with the software?

Software, implementation, hosting, training and ongoing support are distinct parts of your solution. Contact Navari for a proposal based on your scope. We do not publish a single price for every business.

Can we improve an existing ERPNext system?

Yes. Discuss your existing setup, pain points and priorities with the team to identify a suitable customization or support approach.

Do all Frappe apps share data automatically?

Integration needs depend on the applications, versions and workflows involved. Navari will assess those requirements with you instead of assuming every app is connected out of the box.
